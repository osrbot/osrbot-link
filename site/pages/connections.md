# Interfaces and connections

<strong>Control computer</strong> is the computer connected to the sharing device and running OSRBOT Link. The <strong>controlled device</strong> is the equipment you want to operate. Each expansion port belongs to the equipment on its own side.

<figure class="interface-photo"><img src="/manual/document-interface-host.png" alt="OSRBOT Link illustration" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Top view · Control computer side</figcaption></figure>

## Control computer plug

Insert the USB-A plug directly into the control computer, which runs OSRBOT Link.

## Control computer USB 3.0 expansion port

The blue port on top adds a USB port to the control computer for peripherals such as a USB drive or a keyboard/mouse receiver.

<figure class="interface-photo"><img src="/manual/document-interface-controlled.png" alt="OSRBOT Link illustration" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Front view of the two-port end · Controlled device side</figcaption></figure>

## Controlled device USB 2.0 expansion port

The upper port at the two-port end connects USB peripherals to the controlled device.

## Controlled device connection port

Use the supplied USB-A to USB-A data cable to connect the lower port at the two-port end to a USB port on the controlled device.

# OSRBOT Link

Keyboard and Mouse Sharing Device

Zero setup on the controlled device. One cable. Instant control.

Run OSRBOT Link on the control computer and connect the controlled device by USB to operate it with your own keyboard and mouse. Add a capture card to view its screen on your computer, making on-site work with robots, development boards and industrial equipment easier.

## Use and capture-card options

Video features require a capture card. Buy one from OSRBOT or choose your own according to your image-quality, frame-rate and interface needs.

-   The controlled device must support USB keyboard and mouse input.
-   Low latency refers to the keyboard and mouse input path. Video specifications and delay depend on the capture card and video path.
-   Viewing, screenshots, screen recording and GIF capture require a capture card.

## Applications

### Robots and single-board computers

Work with platforms such as Raspberry Pi and Jetson that accept USB keyboard and mouse input, at a workbench or beside the robot.

### Industrial computers and maintenance

Operate the controlled device from your laptop. To view its screen, connect a capture card to the device's video output.

### Teaching and process recording

Use live video, screenshots, video recordings and GIFs to document procedures for teaching and troubleshooting.

### Keyboard and Mouse Sharing Device

For users who already have a screen-viewing solution or only need keyboard and mouse control. Includes a USB-A to USB-A data cable.

### Video capture card

For users who already have a sharing device or only need video capture. Choose by resolution, frame rate, interfaces and appearance.

### Sharing device and capture card bundle

Includes the equipment for keyboard/mouse control and video viewing, with accessories supplied according to the selected bundle.

### Buy a capture card from OSRBOT or choose your own

Choose according to the video input interface, actual USB capture output resolution and frame rate, and control-computer compatibility. A capture card's input specifications may differ from its actual capture output.
